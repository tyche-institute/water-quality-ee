## Long version (Facebook Page, LinkedIn personal)

🌊 Launching **H2O Atlas** — the interactive water-quality map of Estonia.

Every public swimming spot, drinking-water network, pool and water source
that Terviseamet monitors — all 2,199 locations, 69,000+ samples — on one
map, in one click.

What we built:
🔹 Live map of every monitored water site in Estonia
🔹 Official compliance status from Terviseamet open data
🔹 Machine-learning risk assessment (4 models: LR, RF, GB, LightGBM) for
   places where the latest sample tells a different story than the chemistry
🔹 Open source, open data, no ads, no tracking

Why it exists: regulators publish the data, but it lives in XML files few
people open. Citizens want to know if the water at their swimming spot or
in their tap is safe. We bridged the gap.

This is a TalTech Machine Learning master's project (2026), built openly.
The model is decision-support, not medical advice — always trust the
official Terviseamet verdict when in doubt.

🔗 https://h2oatlas.ee
📊 Source data: vtiav.sm.ee
💻 Code: github.com/tyche-institute/water-quality-ee
